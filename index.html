Research

Pixel heatmaps show where a model looks. They rarely say what it recognized. My PhD works at the level of human concepts, such as pedestrian, lane marking or occlusion, so engineers and safety reviewers can check a perception model in their own terms.

  • Concept attributionLocating and quantifying human-interpretable concepts inside CNNs and vision transformers.
  • CounterfactualsMinimal, concept-localized image edits that flip a classifier's decision.
  • XAI evaluationFaithfulness, robustness and localization metrics for explanation methods.
  • DomainPerception for autonomous driving: detection, segmentation, safety review.
